# TechCoder

TechCoder is a technology and lifestyle platform: clear, trustworthy writing on
the technology shaping how we build, work, and live, without the noise.

- **Domain:** [techcoder.tech](https://techcoder.tech)
- **What you'll find:** technical articles, AI news and explainers, honest
  gadget and product reviews, and programming guides and tutorials.
- **Who it's for:** developers, students, and curious tech enthusiasts who want
  reliable, well-explained takes on technology. Readers can optionally sign in
  to like articles and join the discussion.
